Ultimate Guide to Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning Costs & Checklists

Whether you're a renter trying to get your security deposit back, a homeowner preparing to sell, or a cleaning business owner pricing out a job, understanding the nuances of move-in and move-out cleaning is essential. The process of transitioning a property from one occupant to another demands a meticulous approach to cleanliness that goes far beyond standard weekly maintenance. Let's delve deep into the aspects of a move-out clean.

Why Move-Out Cleans Cost More

Many clients are often surprised by the cost of a move-out clean compared to a standard recurring clean. The reality is that an empty house reveals dirt, grime, and hidden messes that are typically concealed by furniture and rugs. Baseboards, the insides of cabinets, and the areas behind large appliances require significant attention.

  • Accumulated Grime: Areas that haven't been touched in years (like behind the stove) take time to degrease and sanitize.
  • Detail Work: Cleaning inside cabinets, drawers, and closets requires wiping down every surface and corner.
  • Higher Expectations: Landlords and new buyers have an expectation of a pristine, "like-new" condition.

Furthermore, without furniture to obstruct the view, imperfections such as scuff marks on baseboards, dust accumulated on ceiling fans, and residue on hard-to-reach ledges become painfully obvious. Cleaning professionals must invest considerably more elbow grease and time to restore these surfaces to their original, flawless state. The products used may also need to be industrial-strength to cut through years of grease and grime built up in the kitchen.

The Standard Move-Out Cleaning Checklist

A comprehensive move-out clean should cover every room from top to bottom. Here is a baseline of what is typically included in a professional service:

Kitchen

  • Wiping down all countertops and backsplashes
  • Cleaning the sink and shining the faucet
  • Wiping down the exterior of all appliances (stove, fridge, dishwasher)
  • Cleaning inside and outside of the microwave
  • Wiping down the inside and outside of all cabinets and drawers
  • Sweeping and mopping the floor

Bathrooms

  • Scrubbing and sanitizing the toilet, tub, and shower
  • Removing soap scum and hard water stains
  • Cleaning the sink and vanity
  • Wiping down the mirrors and fixtures
  • Cleaning inside any vanity cabinets
  • Sweeping and mopping the floors

Living Areas & Bedrooms

  • Dusting ceiling fans and light fixtures
  • Wiping down baseboards, window sills, and door frames
  • Vacuuming all carpets and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping all hard floors
  • Removing cobwebs from corners and ceilings

Common Add-Ons and Upsells

While the standard checklist is thorough, there are several tasks that are typically charged as add-ons due to the extra time and labor they require. These include:

  • Inside the Refrigerator: Requires removing shelves, washing them down, and sanitizing the interior walls to remove spills and odors.
  • Inside the Oven: A highly labor-intensive task that involves heavy degreasers to remove baked-on grease and food particles.
  • Interior Windows: Cleaning the inside glass of all windows to remove smudges, fingerprints, and dust.
  • Deep Baseboard Cleaning: Hand-wiping every inch of baseboard to remove scuffs and embedded dirt.
  • Carpet Shampooing: Deep cleaning carpets using specialized extraction machines to remove deep-seated dirt and stains.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a move-out cleaning typically cost?

Move-out cleaning costs generally range from $150 to $400 depending on the size and condition of the property. Larger homes or properties that require deep cleaning can cost more.

What is included in a standard move-out clean?

A standard move-out clean usually includes dusting, vacuuming, mopping, wiping down counters, and thoroughly cleaning the bathrooms and kitchen. It prepares the home for the next occupant.

Do I need to clean inside appliances?

Cleaning inside the fridge and oven are typically considered add-ons and are not part of a standard move-out clean unless specifically requested. These tasks require extra time and specialized degreasers.
