5 Best Practices for Cleaning Client SMS

  1. Keep Messages Under 160 Characters: Standard single SMS segments contain up to 160 characters. Keeping messages concise guarantees single-segment delivery and prevents carrier fragmentation.
  2. Always Identify Cleaner and Company Name: Clients receive dozens of automated alerts daily. Stating your business name and technician name builds immediate safety and security trust.
  3. Include Frictionless Direct Links: When requesting payment or reviews, always paste short direct links (e.g., Venmo, Zelle, or Google Business review URLs) so clients can take immediate action in one tap.
  4. Never Hide Traffic Delays: If unexpected highway accidents delay your crew, notify the homeowner immediately with an updated arrival ETA rather than arriving late unannounced.
  5. Observe TCPA Legal Hours: Strictly avoid sending automated messages before 8:00 AM or after 8:30 PM local client time to comply with telemarketing and privacy regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to send cleaning appointment reminders?
The industry gold standard is 24 hours prior to the scheduled visit, dispatched between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Sending an ETA text 20 to 30 minutes before arrival on the day of service reduces customer lockouts by over 42%.
How do I ask for a 5-star Google review without being pushy?
Dispatch a short, polite SMS 2 to 4 hours after completing the clean while the fresh scent and spotless home are top of mind. Emphasize how much reviews mean to your small local business and provide a direct 1-tap link.
What should I do if a client does not reply to an arrival notice?
Proceed to the residence at the scheduled time. If access codes fail or doors remain locked upon arrival, place a polite phone call and wait for the contractually agreed grace period (typically 15 minutes) before logging a lockout fee.
Is SMS better than email for residential cleaning clients?
Yes. SMS delivers a 98% open rate compared to 18% to 22% for email in home services. Urgent operational updates (such as arrival notifications and same-day payment requests) should always be handled via text.
