DEADLY COMBINATION — DO NOT MIX!
Mixing Bleach and Vinegar (Acid) releases Chlorine Gas. Inhaling chlorine gas causes chemical burns to the eyes, nose, throat, and lungs, and can be fatal even in moderate concentrations.

Deadly Combinations Quick Reference

Every professional housekeeper and custodian must memorize these 4 lethal interactions:

Combination Hazardous Reaction Severity Physical Symptoms
Bleach + Vinegar / Acids Produces Chlorine Gas FATAL Severe coughing, fluid in lungs, chemical burns
Bleach + Ammonia Produces Chloramine Gas FATAL Shortness of breath, chest pain, coma
Bleach + Rubbing Alcohol Produces Chloroform & Acetone TOXIC Dizziness, loss of consciousness, organ damage
Bleach + Hydrogen Peroxide Violent Oxygen Off-Gassing EXPLOSIVE Sealed bottle ruptures/bursts, severe chemical splash
🚨 Accidental Exposure Protocol
If toxic fumes are accidentally released during a clean:
  1. Evacuate immediately: Get all occupants and pets into fresh outdoor air.
  2. Do not stay to clean up: Opening windows is only advised if you can do so on your way out without inhaling vapors.
  3. Call Emergency: In the US, call Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) or 911 immediately.
