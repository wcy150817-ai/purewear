How to Calculate Tips Based on Hours Worked (The Math)

When cleaning crews work varying shift lengths, splitting tips equally is unfair to cleaners who worked 8 hours versus someone who only worked 3 hours. Using the hours-weighted tip pool method is the legal, transparent standard:

The Exact Formula:

1. Hourly Tip Rate = Total Tip Pool ($) ÷ Total Team Hours
2. Employee Payout = Hourly Tip Rate × Employee's Individual Hours

Real Example:

Suppose your 3-person team receives a $180 tip on a deep-cleaning contract.

  • Sarah worked 8 hours
  • Carlos worked 6 hours
  • Anna worked 4 hours
  • Total Hours = 8 + 6 + 4 = 18 hours
  • Tip Rate = $180 ÷ 18 = $10.00 / hour

Therefore: Sarah receives $80 (8 × $10), Carlos receives $60 (6 × $10), and Anna receives $40 (4 × $10). Every penny is accounted for with zero disputes.
