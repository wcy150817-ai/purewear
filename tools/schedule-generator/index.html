The 3 Golden Rules of Custodial Shift Scheduling

Overtime Legal Formula (FLSA Compliance):
Regular Pay = Total Hours (up to 40) × Hourly Rate
Overtime Pay = Hours in Excess of 40 × (Hourly Rate × 1.5)
Total Gross Payroll = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay
1. Cap Physical Scrubbing

Never schedule more than 7.5 hours of active physical scrubbing per day. High fatigue triples worker injury and property damage.

2. Pair High & Low Mobility

Schedule intense move-out cleans in the morning when cleaners have peak energy, reserving light dusting for afternoons.

3. Route Clustering

Cluster homes within a 5-mile zip code radius per team to eliminate wasted driving hours and vehicle wear.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How far in advance should cleaning business schedules be posted?
Best industry practice is to distribute the following week's schedule by Friday 12:00 PM. This gives cleaners over 48 hours notice before Monday morning shifts and significantly reduces last-minute call-outs and route disruption.
How many hours should a residential cleaner work per day?
Most professional residential cleaners work between 6 and 8 on-site hours per day (typically 2 to 3 average homes). Scheduling beyond 8 physical cleaning hours frequently leads to fatigue, missed details, and higher employee turnover.
How do I avoid overtime wage penalties for cleaners?
Under the US Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), all non-exempt employees must be paid 1.5 times their regular rate for any hours exceeding 40 in a 7-day workweek. This generator highlights any cleaner exceeding 40 hours in red to help managers distribute shifts evenly before locking the schedule.
