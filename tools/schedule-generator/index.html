3 Scheduling Rules to Prevent Cleaner Burnout

1. Cluster Appointments by Zip Code (Route Clustering)
Never dispatch a cleaner across town between consecutive jobs. Grouping homes within a 5-mile radius saves each cleaner 45–60 minutes of unpaid vehicle transit every single day.
2. Flag Overtime (40+ Hours) Early
Our schedule matrix highlights weekly totals exceeding 40 hours in amber. Tracking hours mid-week prevents unexpected 1.5x overtime labor charges on weekend shifts.
3. Pair Inexperienced Cleaners on Deep Cleans
Heavy scrubbing tasks (tile grout, range hoods, windows) are twice as fast with a 2-person team. Pair rookie cleaners with seasoned veterans to maintain consistent quality scores.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I post cleaning schedules?
Publish schedules at least 48 to 72 hours in advance (ideally every Friday at noon for the upcoming week). Advance notice reduces tardiness, prevents double-booking, and gives team members time to arrange childcare.
How do I share this schedule with cleaners on WhatsApp or SMS?
Click "📋 Copy Text" above the schedule table. It formats each team member's daily shifts and total weekly hours into a clean, easy-to-read text message ready to paste directly into your team group chat.
What is the standard overtime pay rate for cleaners?
Under FLSA labor standards in the US, non-exempt hourly employees who exceed 40 hours in a 7-day workweek must be paid 1.5 times (time-and-a-half) their regular base rate. Use our Hourly Pay Calculator to calculate exact overtime paychecks.
