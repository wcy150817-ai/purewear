Best Practices for Scheduling Residential & Commercial Cleaning Teams

Effective shift scheduling is the key to minimizing cleaner turnover, avoiding burnout, and ensuring punctuality on every job site.

1. Allow 30-Minute Transit Buffers

For residential maids and mobile crews, never schedule appointments back-to-back without travel buffers. Factoring in 30 minutes between appointments accounts for traffic, parking, and packing cleaning chemicals safely.

2. Balancing Heavy Deep Cleans

Deep cleaning and post-construction turnovers demand nearly twice the physical stamina of routine maintenance. Avoid assigning the same crew more than two deep cleans consecutively in a single week.
