Quoting window cleaning by "number of windows" is the fastest way to lose money on a residential estate or commercial office complex. To a homeowner, a massive three-panel bay window, a 16-lite true divided colonial French door, and a standard bathroom slider are each "one window." If you quote a flat $12 per window over the phone without counting individual glass panes, storm inserts, screen washing, deep sill/track vacuuming, and 2nd/3rd-story ladder or water-fed pole (WFP) setups, your effective hourly yield can plunge from $110/man-hour to under $38/man-hour.

3. Story Height Multipliers, Water-Fed Poles (DI/RO) & Minimum Trip Charges

Modern pure-water Reverse Osmosis / Deionized (RO/DI) carbon-fiber water-fed pole systems allow a single technician to scrub and spot-free rinse 2nd-, 3rd-, and 4th-story exterior windows from the ground at 000 PPM Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) without setting heavy sectional or extension ladders. However, you should never discount your high-reach per-pane price just because your water-fed pole cleans faster:

  • 1-Story Ranch / Ground-Level Commercial (1.00x Multiplier): Fast walk-around access with zero ladder repositioning or pole extension necks.
  • 2-Story Standard Residential (1.15x Multiplier): Accounts for 24-foot ladder setups, stabilizer standoffs over gutters, or 27-foot carbon-fiber water-fed pole neck fatigue and pure-water DI resin consumption.
  • 3-Story / Walkout Basement / Steep Hillside Grade (1.30x to 1.45x Multiplier): Requires 32-to-40-foot ladders, two-person spotter safety compliance, or high-modulus 40-foot WFP poles plus interior 16-foot A-frame foyer ladders for vaulted chandelier windows.
  • Minimum Residential Job Floor ($175 – $250): Never roll a truck for a residential job under $175 (or $45–$65 for a dense commercial storefront route stop) due to windshield travel time, hose unrolling, and setup overhead.

4. Hard-Water Restoration & Post-Construction Glass Scraping Rules

Two specialty window services generate 40%+ net margins when priced as separate line items—and bankrupt unaware contractors when bundled for free:

  1. Hard-Water Calcium &Silica Stain Restoration ($15 – $45/pane): Lawn irrigation sprinklers spray mineral-heavy groundwater onto lower-story glass, baking calcium carbonate and silica into the microscopic pores of the glass under UV heat. Standard squeegee soap will not touch it. Always perform a Paid Test Pane first and bill $15–$25/pane for Stage-1 acid/0000-steel-wool treatment or $35–$65/pane for Stage-2 rotary cerium oxide polishing.
  2. Post-Construction Window Cleaning (2.0x to 2.5x Base Rate + Scratch Waiver): Removing painter's overspray, stucco splatter, mortar, and factory sticker adhesive requires 4-inch razor scrapers and triple-pass washing. Because poor-quality heat-treated tempered glass often suffers from fabricating debris (microscopic glass roller nibs fused during tempering that scratch when scraped), always require the builder or homeowner to sign an IWCA Fabricating Debris & Tempered Glass Scratch Waiver before touching a single pane with a razor blade.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (Window Cleaning Pricing & Bidding)

How much should I charge per window pane vs per window unit in 2026?

In 2026, professional window cleaners price by individual glass pane rather than whole window assemblies to avoid underbidding large double-hung or triple-slider units. The national benchmark for standard residential glass is $4.50 to $8.00 per pane for inside-and-outside cleaning ($9.00 to $16.00 for a standard 2-pane double-hung window). Exterior-only cleaning typically bills at 55% to 60% of the full inside/outside rate, while interior-only cleaning bills at 45% to 50%.

How do you price French windows, storm windows, and screen washing?

True divided-light French panes (small cut-up glass squares) are priced at $1.25 to $2.50 per individual small pane inside and out because each square requires four separate detailing edges. Traditional triple-track aluminum storm windows require disassembly and four glass surfaces per opening, commanding $18.00 to $30.00 per window assembly. Basic screen brushing/rinsing adds $3.00 to $4.00 per screen, while deep tube-washer screen scrubbing adds $5.00 to $8.00 per screen.

What is the target hourly revenue per technician for residential vs route commercial window cleaning?

Residential window cleaning businesses target $85.00 to $135.00 per man-hour using traditional mop-and-squeegee work inside and Deionized (DI) / Reverse Osmosis (RO) carbon-fiber water-fed poles up to 3 stories outside. High-frequency ground-floor commercial storefront route work (weekly or bi-weekly restaurants and retail strips) targets $65.00 to $90.00 per man-hour due to high route density and zero ladder setups.

How much extra should window cleaners charge for hard-water stain removal and post-construction glass scraping?

Never include sprinkler hard-water calcium stain removal or post-construction silicone/stucco scraping in a standard maintenance window cleaning quote. Stage-1 mineral oxidation removal using mild acid paste or 0000 steel wool bills at $15.00 to $25.00 extra per affected pane, while Stage-2 machine polishing with cerium oxide compounds bills at $35.00 to $65.00 per pane. Post-construction window detailing multiplies your base inside/outside rate by 2.0x to 2.5x and requires a signed Tempered Glass Scratch Waiver.
