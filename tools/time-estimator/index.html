Cleaning Time Estimator

ISSA 612 Standards Verified Created by PureWear Productivity Team • Updated March 2026

Underestimating job duration is the leading cause of technician burnout, late arrivals to subsequent appointments, and compressed profit margins in the residential cleaning industry. On the other hand, overestimating hours results in inflated quotes that lose competitive bids.

This cleaning time estimator is calibrated according to ISSA 612 Cleaning Times, the worldwide gold standard for custodial labor production rates. It translates home square footage, room count, service scope, and crew size into exact on-site duration and billing man-hours.

The ISSA Production Rate Formula

Base Labor Hours:
Base Man-Hours = 1.20 + (Cleanable Sq Ft ÷ 1,400) + (Beds × 0.25h) + (Baths × 0.50h)

Adjusted Total Man-Hours:
Total Man-Hours = (Base Man-Hours × Service Tier Multiplier × Condition Multiplier) + (Add-on Minutes ÷ 60)

On-Site Duration (Clock Time):
On-Site Hours = Total Man-Hours ÷ Crew Size

4 Key Rules for Accurate Time Estimates

  1. Scale Duration by Crew Size, Not Man-Hours: Sending 2 cleaners instead of 1 cuts the on-site duration in half (e.g., a 4-hour solo job takes 2 hours with 2 pros), but total billable man-hours remain 4.0.
  2. Add a 45-Minute Buffer Between Appointments: Equipment loading, transit traffic, client walkthroughs, and safety sanitization require at least 30–45 minutes of scheduled buffer between route stops.
  3. Bathrooms Are the Primary Labor Bottleneck: Hand-scrubbing tile grout, shower glass limescale, and toilet sanitization accounts for over 50% of active physical scrubbing time.
  4. Heavy Pet Hair Adds 25% Time: Homes with shedding dogs or cats require double-pass HEPA vacuuming on upholstery and baseboard dusting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take one cleaner to clean a 2,000 sq ft house?
For a standard recurring maintenance cleaning (dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen, and 2.5 bathrooms), one solo cleaner typically requires 3.5 to 4.5 hours. If it is a first-time deep clean or move-out clean, it requires 6.0 to 8.0 man-hours.
How does crew size affect total on-site cleaning time?
Total man-hours remain roughly constant, but on-site duration scales inversely with crew size. For example, a 6 man-hour job takes 6 hours for a solo cleaner, 3 hours for a 2-person crew, and 2 hours for a 3-person crew.
Why does a move-out or deep clean take nearly double standard time?
Deep cleans involve labor-intensive tasks excluded from routine maintenance: scrubbing inside kitchen appliances (ovens, refrigerators), wiping inside empty cabinets and drawers, cleaning window tracks, and washing baseboards and door casings by hand.
