Industry Benchmarks for Cleaning Time

Professional cleaning standards generally follow a baseline productivity rate of 400–600 sq ft per man-hour for maintenance cleanings, dropping to 200–350 sq ft per man-hour for deep cleanings and move-out turns.

• Bedrooms & Living Areas: Average 15–25 mins each (dusting, making beds, vacuuming).
