How Custodial Inventory Controls Maximize Cash Flow

In cleaning businesses, supply expenses average 5% to 8% of total revenue. Running out of critical supplies like disinfectant or commercial trash liners mid-route forces technicians into expensive retail store emergency purchases that destroy job margins.

The Custodial Par Level Formula

📐 CUSTODIAL PAR LEVEL & REORDER TRIGGER EQUATIONS
\text{Par Level (Units)} = (\text{Daily Usage Rate} \times \text{Lead Time Days}) + \text{Safety Buffer Stock}
\text{Reorder Trigger Quantity} = \text{Lead Time Consumption} + \text{Emergency Minimum}
\text{Stockout Expense Leakage} = \text{Retail Unit Cost} - \text{Commercial Wholesale Cost}
Custodial Supply Chain Benchmarks:
• Supply Cost Target: Chemical and disposable supplies should average 4.5% to 6.5% of gross residential cleaning revenue.
• Retail Surcharge Penalty: Emergency retail store purchases (e.g. buying 32oz disinfectant at $6.50 vs $0.45 RTU concentrate) erode job gross margins by up to 14%.
• Weekly Restock Rhythm: Inventory audit performed every Friday afternoon ensures vans are fully provisioned before Monday morning dispatch.
