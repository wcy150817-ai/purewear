How Overtime Pay is Calculated

Under the U.S. Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and labor regulations in most jurisdictions, covered non-exempt employees must receive overtime pay for hours worked over 40 in a workweek at a rate not less than one and one-half times (1.5x) their regular rate of pay.

Calculation Formula:

Total Gross Pay = (Regular Hours × Base Rate) + (Overtime Hours × Base Rate × Multiplier)

For cleaning companies, calculating accurate overtime is essential when billing clients for emergency jobs, post-construction cleans, or weekend commercial maintenance.
