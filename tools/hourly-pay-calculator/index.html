How Overtime Pay is Calculated

Under the U.S. Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and labor regulations in most countries, covered non-exempt hourly employees must receive overtime pay for hours worked over 40 in a workweek at a rate not less than one and one-half times (1.5x) their regular rate of pay.

Calculation Formula:

Regular Pay = Regular Hours × Base Hourly Rate
Overtime Pay = Overtime Hours × Base Hourly Rate × Multiplier (1.5)
Total Gross Earnings = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay
Estimated Net Pay = Gross Earnings − (Gross Earnings × Tax Rate %)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is 1.5x overtime calculated?
Multiply your hourly base rate by 1.5. For example, if you earn $20.00/hour, your overtime rate is $30.00/hour ($20 × 1.5). If you work 5 overtime hours, your overtime earnings equal $150.00 ($30 × 5).
Can an employer average hours across two weeks to avoid paying overtime?
No. Under FLSA rules, each workweek stands alone. An employer cannot average 50 hours in Week 1 and 30 hours in Week 2 to claim an 80-hour bi-weekly period without paying 10 hours of overtime for Week 1.
How do tips affect cleaner paychecks?
Tips are treated as supplemental income. While employers cannot take employee tips to satisfy base wages, tips must be reported for income tax purposes. Use our Tip Split Calculator to divide team tips fairly.
