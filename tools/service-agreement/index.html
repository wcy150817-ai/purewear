Residential Cleaning Service Agreement

1. Parties & Property

This Service Agreement is entered into between PureWear Cleaning Co. ("Provider") and Jane Doe ("Client"). Services will be performed at: 123 Elm Street, City, ST.

2. Scope & Frequency

Provider agrees to furnish custodial supplies and labor for scheduled cleaning on a Bi-Weekly basis starting on October 1, 2026.

3. Compensation & Billing

Client agrees to pay $180.00 per completed visit. Payment is due on the day of service upon completion.

4. Cancellation & Lockout Policy

Client must provide at least 24 hours advance notice to reschedule or cancel. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or inability to access the premises may be assessed a 50% lockout fee.

5. Safe Access & Household Pets

Client agrees to ensure safe property access. Friendly pets are permitted, but aggressive animals must be safely crated or secured during service hours.

6. Limitation of Liability & Accidental Damage

Provider maintains general commercial liability insurance. Any accidental damage will be reported to Client within 24 hours. Provider is not responsible for pre-existing surface wear, unstable wall decor, or irreplaceable sentimental items.

Why Every Cleaning Business Needs Written Agreements

Verbal handshakes often lead to unpaid lockouts, disputes over pet damage, and scope creep (e.g., expecting oven scrubbing without extra compensation). A simple, transparent 1-page agreement sets mutual expectations before cleaners step through the front door.
