Residential Cleaning Service Agreement

Effective Date:

1. Parties & Property

This Cleaning Service Agreement is made between ("Provider") and ("Client").

The service will be performed at the following residential premises: .

2. Service Scope & Frequency

The Provider agrees to furnish standard professional cleaning supplies and labor for scheduled visits on a basis commencing on .

3. Compensation & Billing

The Client agrees to pay the Provider per completed cleaning visit.

Payment is due upon completion of each visit unless a monthly invoicing term has been pre-authorized in writing.

4. Cancellation & Lockout Policy

Because schedule slots are reserved in advance, Client agrees to provide at least 24 hours advance notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or inability to access the property may be subject to a 50% lockout fee.

5. Safe Access & Pets

Client is responsible for providing safe access (key, lockbox, or presence). If pets are in the residence, friendly animals are permitted but aggressive pets must be confined in a secure room or crate during service hours.

6. Limitation of Liability & Breakage

The Provider maintains general liability insurance. The Provider will notify Client within 24 hours of any accidental damage caused by staff. Provider is not liable for pre-existing surface defects, normal wear and tear, unanchored wall hangings, or items of high irreplaceable sentimental value.
