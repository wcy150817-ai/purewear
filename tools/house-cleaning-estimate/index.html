How to Price House Cleaning Accurately in 2026

Getting a fair and accurate house cleaning estimate has never been more critical. As the cost of living and professional services evolve in 2026, understanding exactly what you're paying for—and how those costs are calculated—can save you both time and money.

The Core Factors of Cleaning Estimates

Most professional cleaning companies and independent maids calculate their rates based on a few foundational metrics:

  • Square Footage (Sq Ft): This is the most reliable baseline for how much ground needs to be covered. Larger homes simply require more sweeping, mopping, and vacuuming.
  • Bedrooms & Bathrooms: Bathrooms and kitchens are notoriously the most time-consuming rooms to clean. A 1,500 sq ft home with 1 bathroom will cost less to clean than a 1,500 sq ft home with 3 bathrooms due to the intensive scrubbing required.
  • Cleaning Type (Standard vs. Deep): A standard clean maintains a home’s cleanliness, while a deep clean tackles built-up grime, baseboards, interior windows, and inside appliances. Deep cleans generally take 50% longer.

Standard vs. Deep Clean: What's the Difference?

When you book a Standard Clean, expect dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and basic bathroom/kitchen wipe-downs. It is designed for homes that are already regularly maintained.

A Deep Clean goes beyond the surface. It involves scrubbing tile grout, wiping down ceiling fans, cleaning inside the oven and refrigerator, and washing blinds. If it's been more than a month since your last professional cleaning, most services will require a deep clean as the initial service.
