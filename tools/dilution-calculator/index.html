Dilution Rules & Safety Guidelines

Golden Rule: "Always Add Chemical to Water, Never Water to Chemical"
Adding water to pure concentrate can cause concentrated splashes or violent foaming. Always fill your bottle or bucket with water first, then add the measured concentrate.

The Universal Dilution Formula:

Total Parts = Ratio Number + 1
Concentrate Needed = Total Volume ÷ Total Parts
Water Needed = Total Volume − Concentrate Needed

Proper dilution protects flooring, acrylic tubs, and delicate countertops from chemical burns while saving your business thousands of dollars in chemical waste.
