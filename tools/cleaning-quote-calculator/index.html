How to Accurately Price House Cleaning Jobs in 2026

Pricing cleaning services requires balancing profitability with competitive market rates. Charging too little burns out your staff, while charging too high drives clients to competitors.

1. Square Footage vs. Hourly Pricing

Most professional cleaning businesses charge by square footage for regular recurring homes. It rewards speed and experienced teams:

  • Standard Upkeep ($0.10 - $0.15/sq ft): Ideal for homes cleaned every 1-2 weeks. Covers vacuuming, mopping, kitchen counters, and sanitizing bathrooms.
  • Deep Clean ($0.16 - $0.20/sq ft): For first-time clients or spring cleaning. Includes scrubbing grout, door frames, dusting ceiling fans, and scale removal.
  • Move-In/Move-Out ($0.20 - $0.28/sq ft): Empty residences requiring deep degreasing, wiping out cabinets, baseboards, and closet shelving.

2. Why Bathrooms Carry Surcharges

Bathrooms consume over 40% of the total labor time in any residential job due to shower scum, toilets, and mirrors. If a home has more than 2 bathrooms, adding a $20 to $30 surcharge per extra bathroom protects your hourly margins.
