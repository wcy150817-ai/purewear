How to Quote House Cleaning: The 4-Step Formula

Step 1: Calculate Base Square Footage Rate
Most professional cleaning companies charge a baseline rate of $0.09 to $0.15 per square foot for routine recurring jobs.
Step 2: Factor in Bathrooms & Kitchens
Bathrooms require 3x more labor than bedrooms due to grout descaling, glass treatment, and sanitization. Always add a $15–$25 per-bathroom labor surcharge.
Step 3: Charge 40%–60% More for Initial Deep Cleanings
Never offer recurring discount pricing on the first appointment. The initial visit requires clearing months of accumulated dust and grease before routine maintenance can begin.
Step 4: Lock In Long-Term Revenue with Frequency Discounts
Offer 15% off for bi-weekly clients and 20% off for weekly clients. Recurring clients reduce travel costs and eliminate client acquisition expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I charge per square foot for house cleaning?
In 2026, standard maintenance cleaning averages $0.09 to $0.15 per square foot. Deep cleaning ranges from $0.15 to $0.22/sq ft, while move-in/move-out turns range between $0.20 and $0.28/sq ft.
Should I charge hourly or flat rate for cleaning?
Over 78% of homeowners prefer flat-rate quotes because there are no surprises on the invoice. Flat pricing also rewards fast, skilled cleaners who complete high-quality jobs without penalizing their hourly take-home pay.
How do I quote extra dirty or hoarder homes?
For extreme clutter or pet hair, apply a 25% to 50% condition surcharge. Always walk through the property or request video walk-throughs before finalizing quotes for first-time clients.
