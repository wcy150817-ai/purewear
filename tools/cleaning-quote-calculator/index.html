The 4-Step Professional Quoting Blueprint

Standard Cleaning Rate Formula:
Base Rate = (Cleanable Sq Ft × Base $/sqft) + (Bathrooms × $25) + (Bedrooms × $10)
Adjusted Rate = Base Rate × Service Tier Multiplier × Dirt Level Multiplier
Final Quote = Adjusted Rate + Optional Add-on Services
1. Establish Floor Rate

Never bid below your operational breakeven ($35–$45/labor hour minimum) regardless of home size.

2. Bathroom Weighting

Bathrooms take 60% of total chemical and scrubbing time. Always charge extra per half or full bath.

3. Upfront Deep Clean

Charge 1.4x to 1.6x on the initial visit to reset the home before locking in discounted recurring prices.

4. Retention Discounts

Offer 15% off bi-weekly or 20% off weekly. High frequency ensures predictable route density.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much should I charge per square foot for house cleaning?
In 2026, standard maintenance cleaning rates range from $0.09 to $0.15 per square foot. Deep cleanings range between $0.15 and $0.22 per square foot, while move-out turnover cleanings average $0.20 to $0.28 per square foot due to appliance detailing and heavy baseboard scrubbing.
Should cleaning contractors charge hourly or flat rate?
Over 78% of residential cleaning companies use flat-rate pricing based on square footage and room count. Homeowners appreciate knowing the exact price upfront without fear of cleaners dragging hours. Flat rates also reward high-speed, well-trained cleaning crews.
How much more should I charge for a first-time deep clean?
First-time cleans require clearing months of built-up soap scum, grease on kitchen cabinets, and dust. You should charge 40% to 60% higher (1.4x–1.6x multiplier) than your standard maintenance rate for the initial visit.
What frequency discounts should I offer to secure recurring cleaning clients?
Standard industry benchmarks recommend 20% off for weekly, 15% off for bi-weekly, and 10% off for monthly service. This encourages clients to sign up for consistent recurring revenue.
