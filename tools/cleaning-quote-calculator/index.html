Cleaning Quote Calculator

ISSA 2026 Calibrated Created by PureWear Operations Desk • Updated March 2026

Whether you run an established custodial service or work as an independent residential cleaner, accurate quoting is the single most important factor determining your business profitability. Bidding too low drains your margins, while bidding blindly above local market rates leads to lost contracts.

This cleaning quote calculator computes standard residential and light commercial bids based on total cleanable square footage, bathroom/bedroom count, initial grime level, and specialized add-on tasks. It also factors in recurring frequency discounts and project labor hour estimates.

The Standard Cleaning Quote Formula

Professional cleaning rates are mathematically derived from baseline production speeds, labor burdens, and supply consumption:

Base Cleaning Quote:
Base Price = (Cleanable Area × Base Rate per Unit) + (Baths × $28.00) + (Beds × $12.00)

Adjusted Client Total:
Quote Total = (Base Price × Service Tier Multiplier × Clutter Multiplier) + Add-ons

Recurring Rates:
Bi-Weekly Rate = Quote Total × 0.85 (15% discount)
Weekly Rate = Quote Total × 0.80 (20% discount)

The 4-Step Professional Quoting Blueprint

To consistently generate profitable bids that close client inquiries, adhere to these operational principles:

  1. Establish a Non-Negotiable Minimum: Never accept a cleaning job below your minimum dispatch cost ($95–$120). Travel time, equipment setup, and insurance apply whether a studio takes 1.5 hours or 3 hours.
  2. Weight Bathrooms Heavily: Bathrooms represent over 60% of total chemical usage and physical scrubbing labor due to soap scum, limescale, and tile grout. Always account for each additional half or full bathroom.
  3. Mandate Initial Deep Cleans: For any new recurring client, perform an initial deep clean at 1.45× to 1.6× baseline before locking in the discounted recurring schedule. This resets the home to a manageable baseline.
  4. Incentivize Route Density: Offering 15% off for bi-weekly visits and 20% off for weekly visits secures predictable cash flow and tightens your geographic travel radius.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I charge per square foot for house cleaning?
In 2026, standard maintenance house cleaning rates range from $0.09 to $0.15 per square foot. Deep cleaning services generally range from $0.15 to $0.22 per square foot, and move-in/move-out turnover cleans range from $0.20 to $0.28 per square foot.
Should cleaning contractors charge hourly or flat rate?
Over 78% of residential homeowners prefer flat-rate pricing based on square footage and room count because it eliminates unexpected billing charges. Flat rates also reward experienced, well-equipped crews who work efficiently without sacrificing quality.
How do I calculate profit margins for a cleaning business?
A sustainable cleaning business targets 25% to 35% net profit after deducting direct field wages (40–45%), cleaning chemicals and disposable microfiber (5–8%), and business overhead including insurance, scheduling software, and fuel (15–20%).
What frequency discounts should I offer to secure recurring cleaning clients?
Industry standards recommend 20% off for weekly service, 15% off for bi-weekly service, and 10% off for monthly service. This predictable cadence ensures steady income and tight route clusters.
