Our Story & Purpose

Starting and running an independent cleaning company or solo housekeeping route is hard work. Between scrubbing tiles, managing client keys, scheduling cleaners, and calculating overtime, business owners shouldn't have to struggle with clunky software or expensive subscriptions.

PureWear Space was created to solve both the operational and physical demands of the cleaning industry. Our free software tools handle the numbers on your phone or laptop, while our workwear store equips your team with comfortable, professional apparel that commands premium client rates.
